# Topic set: what parents avoid, what teens want

## Summary

> **Key finding: the gap.** Parents want to warn about **risks**. Teens want to be heard about **relationships, feelings and friends**. The topics parents have covered most (phone, online safety, body image, consent) are the ones no teen asked for more of.

**Topics families need:**

1. **Core:** relationships and dating · stress and feelings · friends and peer pressure · adult content (inside the relationships talk)
2. **Safety essentials:** online safety · consent · body and growing up · body image
3. **Supporting:** phone and gaming · money · alcohol and vaping · gender and LGBTQ

## 1. What both sides told us

| Topic | Parents: already covered | Parents: putting off | Teens: want more |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relationships and intimacy | 44% | 30% | **83%** |
| Stress and mental health | 67% | 30% | **58%** |
| Friends, peer pressure, crushes | 61% | 30% | **58%** |
| Body and growing up | 61% | 30% | 25% |
| Bullying | 44% | 20% | 25% |
| Adult content online | 39% | **50%** | 17% |
| Gender and LGBTQ | 17% | 0% | 17% |
| Alcohol, smoking, vaping | 50% | 0% | 8% |
| Money and spending | 56% | 20% | 8% |
| Body image | 67% | 20% | 0% |
| Consent and personal safety | 33% | 0% | 0% |
| Online safety | 56% | 0% | 0% |
| Phone, social media, gaming | 67% | 20% | 0% |

*Bases: "covered" is out of 18 parents, "putting off" is out of the 10 parents who are delaying a talk, and "want more" is out of 12 teens. Parents picked one topic they were putting off, and teens picked up to 3. With samples this small, one person moves a figure by 6 to 10 points, so read these as signals. Parents and teens are from different families.*

## 2. The gap: where they disagree

| Parents | Teens | So what |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adult content is the hardest talk: **50%** of parents delaying a talk are delaying this one | **83%** want relationships talk. Only **17%** want more on adult content | Start with relationships, and bring the risk into it |
| Safety topics are covered: phone 67%, body image 67%, online safety 56%, consent 33% | **0%** want more on any of these | These arrive as lectures. Change *how* they are talked about, not how often |
| Stress (67%) and friends (61%) are covered | **58%** still want more of each | Teens don't feel heard. They want depth, not facts |
| Good moments: meals (61%), car rides (44%) | Good moments: walks (**75%**), late night (**58%**). Car rides only **8%** | Teens open up side by side, in private |
| **100%** of fathers of daughters are putting off a talk (all 3) | **0%** picked father as easier to talk to (mother 33%, "depends" 42%) | Fathers need the most help |

## 3. What wider evidence adds

| Topic | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Relationships and sex | 17% to 87% of Indian adolescents have never discussed sexual topics with a parent. Mothers say they lack the words, and boys get less guidance |
| Adult content | 73% of teens have seen porn, over half of them by age 13. Fewer than half have talked about it with a trusted adult |
| Stress | 81% of Indian students say exams cause anxiety. Only 41% of young Indians think seeking mental health help is good |
| Body image | Over 45% of Indian students are unhappy with their body |
| Online safety | 85% of Indian children have faced cyberbullying, and 45% hid it from parents |
| LGBTQ | Indian queer youth fear coming out to family. Family support lowers suicide risk |
| Public voices | Young people describe scolding instead of talks, fear-based warnings, and "just cheer up." Their message to parents: *listen, don't lecture* |

## 4. Final topic set

**Rule:** a topic is core if teens want it, parents struggle with it, or the risk is high. Lead with the teen's need, and fold the parent's fear into it.

| Tier | Topic | Why | How to frame |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | Relationships and dating | Top teen need: 83% want more | "How do you know someone is good for you?" |
| **1** | Stress and feelings | 58% of teens want more. High stigma | Listen first, then solve |
| **1** | Friends and peer pressure | 58% of teens want more | Ask about their world, not rules |
| **1** | Adult content | Parents' top fear: 50% of those delaying a talk | Inside the relationships talk: "what's real vs what's shown" |
| **2** | Online safety (blackmail, fake images, bullying) | High and rising risk. 0% of teens ask for it | "Come to me, you won't be in trouble" |
| **2** | Consent and personal safety | Only 33% of parents have covered it. Most abusers are known to the child | The right to say no to *anyone* |
| **2** | Body and growing up | 30% of delaying parents are putting it off. Missing for boys | Plain facts, for boys too |
| **2** | Body image | 45% of Indian students are unhappy with their body | Fewer comments on looks |
| **3** | Phone and gaming | 67% of parents have covered it. 0% of teens want more | Agree family rules together |
| **3** | Money | Only 8% of teens want more | Fold in gaming and scams |
| **3** | Alcohol and vaping | Only 8% of teens want more. Most teens don't know vapes are banned | One fact card |
| **3** | Gender and LGBTQ | 17% of parents have covered it, and 17% of teens want more. Very high stakes | Gentle support guide |
